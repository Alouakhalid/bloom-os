# 🌸 BLOOM // AESTHETIC LIFE OS v2.0
> **An Aesthetic, Cozy Notion-Style Life Operating System Designed for Mindful Productivity, Self-Care & Deen.**

Theme: Dreamy Rose Gold & Lavender Pastel on Midnight Plum, Sakura Dust Floating Particles, and Gentle Cozy Lo-Fi Vibes.

---

## ⚡ Features
- 🌸 **Daily Bloom HQ**: Daily intentions, priority tasks, 8-cup water hydration tracker, and completion telemetry.
- 🕌 **Spiritual Sanctuary**: 5 Daily Prayers, Quran Khatma tracker, Morning & Evening Adhkar (أذكار الصباح والمساء), Qiyam al-Layl & Tahajjud.
- ☕ **Cozy Study Lounge**: Soft Pomodoro focus timer with lo-fi bell, study subjects with syllabus progress, and auto-saving notes.
- 🎬 **Aesthetic Creator Studio**: 4-stage pipeline for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.
- 🌷 **Goals Matrix**: Projects and milestones tracking.
- ⏰ **12:00 AM Midnight Engine**: Live digital clock with automatic daily reset of prayers, hydration, and daily habits.
- 💾 **Privacy & Backups**: LocalStorage persistence, 1-click JSON backup & restore, and Export to Notion Markdown.
